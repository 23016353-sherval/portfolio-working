VR ESCAPE ROOM

~~INTRODUCTION~~

This project is a VR escape room game that I developed during one of my modules and it was the first time I ever developed a game. The game was created in the Unity engine in the C# lanaguage. We were tasked with creating a VR escape room, we could create whatever we wanted with whatever theme we wanted as long as it was within the requirements.

~~INSPIRATION~~

For the puzzle, I got the inspirationfrom my favourite series 'How To Train Your Dragon: Race To The Edge'. In the series there is an object called the Dragon Eye which shows you a different projection based on the lens you put in and the type of dragon fire that is shining through the Dragon Eye, they slowly find all the lenses and dragons for their fire until they finally reach the last dragon they need to protect from the villains. I thought this was a really cool concept and I wanted to create a similar puzzle in my game.

For the design of the escape room, since I already had that idea for the puzzle I just decided to make it a How To Train Your Dragon themed escape room, to fit the puzzle nicely.

Projection from Dragon Eye using Toothless's Purple Dragon Fire

First 2 show Viking theme, last 2 inspired the layout for the escape room

~~CORE MECHANICS~~

For the core mechanics, for the puzzles, players can click on levers to open doors and solve puzzles, they can also pick up and move objects around the room to compile their information and make it easier to solve the puzzles. For the movement in the escape room, there are teleporter pads at key areas that players use to move around the map, some of the teleporter pad locations might even be hints for the player that something is there.

Teleporter pads around the room

Objects can be picked up and moved around

~~PUZZLE DESIGN~~

For the main puzzle, I tried to do something similar to the Dragon Eye puzzle I mentioned above, There weren't any assets that looked Similar to the Dragon Eye so I used a Dragon Statue instead. The puzzle starts with instructions in a riddle that tells you what to do but the instructions are simple, see the colour of the projection then find the gem with the same colour, once you find the gem, you have to pull the lever with the same shape as the gem. Once all the levers are pulled, the projection turns white and the cheat in the room is unlocked.

Projection is blue, blue gem is a star

Pull the star shaped lever

Projection becomes white

Puzzle completed, chest unlocked

For the last puzzle, there are 6 levers on the wall and you need to pull them in the correct order for the door to open, there is a clue sheet that has questions with numbered answers, and the answers can be found around the entire room, some should be easy since they would have ran around the room to look for gems earlier. Once all the questions are answered that will be the order you have to pull the levers to open the last door and escape the room.

I think the best parts I liked about this project for the puzzles would be the main puzzle, I was able to create a puzzle that kind of resembles the one that I got the inspiration from. For the escape room itself, I am very proud of this entire house that the escape room takes place in because I created the room from scratch since there weren't any houses that fit the layout I had in mind, so for the first time using Unity and coding a game, I'm very proud of the work that I've done.

~~OVERALL~~

Overall this project was very fun and it really opened my eyes into the big wide world of game development, I had no prior experience with game development before this project and I was able to create a VR escape room that I am really proud of. Making a game can be very tiring but the satisfaction of seeing your game come to life and being able to play it is really rewarding and I would love to experience it again. If given the chance to make a game again I would love to do it, and hopefully I can do it better than this one.
